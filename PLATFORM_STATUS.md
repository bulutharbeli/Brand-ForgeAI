## 🎯 How to Use (Step-by-Step)

### 1. Generate a Brand Name
- Open `http://localhost:5000`
- Scroll to "Try It Yourself" section
- Click "🔤 Brand Names" tab
- Select industry: "Technology"
- Enter keywords: `innovative, cloud, smart`
- Select style: "Modern"
- Click "Generate Brand Names"
- **Result**: You'll see 10 AI-generated names like "TechCloud", "SmartAI", etc.

### 2. Create a Color Palette
- Click "🎨 Colors" tab
- Select industry and mood
- Optionally pick a primary color
- Click "Generate Palette"
- **Result**: 8-color palette with usage guidelines

### 3. Generate Logo Concepts
- Click "✏️ Logos" tab
- Enter the brand name you like
- Select industry and style
- Click "Generate Logo Concepts"
- **Result**: 5 different logo concepts (lettermark, wordmark, pictorial, abstract, mascot)

### 4. Create Slogans
- Click "💬 Slogans" tab
- Enter brand name and industry
- Add brand values
- Click "Generate Slogans"
- **Result**: Multiple catchy slogans

### 5. Analyze Competitors
- Click "📊 Analysis" tab
- Enter your industry
- List competitors (comma-separated)
- Click "Analyze Competitors"
- **Result**: Complete competitor analysis with market overview, gaps, and recommendations

### 6. Generate Brand Guidelines
- Click "📋 Guidelines" tab
- Fill in brand details
- Click "Generate Guidelines"
- **Result**: Complete brand style guide

### 7. Save and Manage Brands
- After generating guidelines, the brand is auto-saved
- Visit `/dashboard` to see all saved brands
- Click on a brand to view details
- Export or delete brands as needed
