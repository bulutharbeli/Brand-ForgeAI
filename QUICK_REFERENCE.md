## 📋 STEP-BY-STEP (Copy & Follow)

### ✅ STEP 1: Brand Names
```
1. Open http://localhost:5000
2. Scroll to "TRY IT YOURSELF"
3. Click "🔤 Brand Names"
4. Select: Industry = Technology
5. Type: Keywords = innovative, cloud, smart
6. Select: Style = Modern
7. CLICK "Generate Brand Names"
8. WAIT 2 seconds
9. SEE 10 names → Click your favorite
```
**Result:** You have a brand name! ✅

---

### ✅ STEP 2: Colors
```
1. Click "🎨 Colors"
2. Select: Industry = Technology
3. Select: Mood = Professional
4. CLICK "Generate Palette"
5. WAIT 1 second
6. SEE 8 colors
```
**Result:** You have colors! ✅

---

### ✅ STEP 3: Logos
```
1. Click "✏️ Logos"
2. Type: Brand Name = (your name from Step 1)
3. Select: Industry = Technology
4. Select: Design Style = Minimalist
5. CLICK "Generate Logo Concepts"
6. WAIT 2 seconds
7. SCROLL to see 5 concepts
```
**Result:** You have logo concepts! ✅

---

### ✅ STEP 4: Slogans
```
1. Click "💬 Slogans"
2. Type: Brand Name = (your name)
3. Select: Industry = Technology
4. Type: Brand Values = innovation, trust
5. CLICK "Generate Slogans"
6. WAIT 1 second
7. SEE 7 slogans
```
**Result:** You have slogans! ✅

---

### ✅ STEP 5: Guidelines (SAVES BRAND!)
```
1. Click "📋 Guidelines"
2. Type: Brand Name = (your name)
3. Select: Industry = Technology
4. Type: Selected Slogan = (your favorite)
5. Select: Brand Voice = Professional
6. CLICK "Generate Guidelines"
7. WAIT 3 seconds
8. SCROLL to see complete guide
```
**Result:** Brand AUTO-SAVED! ✅

---

### ✅ STEP 6: Dashboard
```
1. Click "Dashboard" (top navigation)
2. SEE your saved brand(s)
3. CLICK eye icon (👁️) to view
4. CLICK download icon (📥) to export
5. CLICK trash icon (🗑️) to delete
```
**Result:** You can manage brands! ✅

## 🆘 STUCK? QUICK FIXES

### Problem: Page blank
**Fix:** Scroll down! Content is below fold.

### Problem: No names appearing
**Fix:** 
1. Did you select industry?
2. Click "Generate" button (don't just press Enter)
3. Wait 2 seconds

### Problem: "Where's my saved brand?"
**Fix:** You MUST complete Step 5 (Guidelines) to save!

### Problem: Server not running
**Fix:**
```bash
cd /home/user/Brand-ForgeAI
source venv/bin/activate
python app.py
```
